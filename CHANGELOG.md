# Changelog

## [Unpublished]

### Added

- Text templates and collections can be copied and pasted between MDScribe installations, including airgapped ones. "Vorlage kopieren" on a template page and "Sammlung kopieren" in an open collection copy a plain-text exchange format (front matter `mdscribe: template/v1` or `collection/v1`, with content, Informationen and examples between paired `<!-- mdscribe:… -->` markers), and their menu also downloads it as a `.md` file. On the library pages "Einfügen" (or Ctrl/⌘+V outside inputs) accepts pasted text or a file, shows a preview to choose the templates, and saves them as the user's own templates, as a new collection when a collection was pasted. Afterwards the imported template or collection opens. Text cut off while copying is detected and reported.
- The documentation site has three tabs: Dokumentation for clinicians, Betrieb for administrators, and a German markdoc-md section with a tutorial, developer quickstart, concepts, a page per tag, guides (custom components, FHIR sources, citations, editor integration, schema extensions), and API and diagnostics references. The markdoc-md overview opens with an interactive demo that fills a template step by step and then lets readers change the inputs (`apps/docs/snippets/live-template.jsx`), and it and the Textbausteine page embed a short clip of filling a template in MDScribe (`apps/docs/videos/`). Pages show rendered previews, flows, and case ladders built from shared snippets in `apps/docs/snippets/markdoc.jsx`, and every template example was checked against the markdoc-md validator and renderer. The German pages were rewritten against the current app: inline template inputs, the Condition tag, Informationen and field descriptions instead of HTML comments (which render as visible text), correct admin paths and model roles, the protocols and model roles the provider dialog actually offers, Text-Snippet management, and what free and Plus accounts store. The empty Diátaxis index pages and the knowledge placeholder were removed with redirects.
- The template editor keeps `{% cite %}` text as a dotted-underlined mark, as in AI-Scribe answers, so editing a letter or template with citations keeps them with their sources; typing next to a citation does not extend it.
- Admin preview flag `unified-aiscribe`: AI Scribe opens every template in the letter editor. A template without sections becomes one field titled like the template, with both case fields (Hintergrundinformationen and Notizen), "Generieren" and "Text kopieren" (without a title); the model's unframed answer fills that field with its citations, and regenerating it or asking the agent sends its current text as `<entwurf>` to rewrite.
- The case (text, audio and files) is shared by template, AI Scribe and PDF form pages: it lives in the root layout, so switching templates with the header switcher, "Mit KI ausfüllen" or the menubar keeps it, and audio is transcribed once per recording for every template. While the case holds input, a "Fall" chip in the menubar summarizes it and starts a "Neuer Fall"; signing out or reloading the page also ends it. The "Weiterverwenden" box on AI Scribe pages, the encrypted page-to-page context transfer behind it and its `contextTransfers.create`/`consume` routes and the outdated announcement email drafts for "Weiterverwenden", AI Textbausteine and Brief-Baukästen are removed; the phone photo upload keeps its encrypted transfer. The menubar links navigate client-side instead of reloading the page. Template, PDF form and AI Scribe pages show a breadcrumb: the collection ("Alle Vorlagen" or one of the user's collections), switchable from its menu, which also opens the library or collection, then the template switcher with that collection's templates; on AI Scribe pages it switches between AI Scribe pages. The breadcrumb has no separate collection-assignment button; templates are added to or removed from a collection on its page. The author and date next to it use the regular small text size.
- The letter editor (a template with two or more sections) has one "Fall-Eingabe" with Hintergrundinformationen, audio and files. "Brief generieren" writes every section in one request and shows each section as a proposal to accept or reject. Each section's ✨ sends the same whole case input: text, uploaded files as their OCR text, and audio, as its transcript once a generation has transcribed it (`scribeStream` takes `audioTranscripts`, at most one per allowed recording and 100,000 characters each, and returns new transcripts as `data-audio-transcripts` only when there is exactly one per recording, so a combined transcription is never reused for a single recording); the documentation agent uses the same Hintergrundinformationen. "Brief kopieren" copies all sections under their titles, and "Vorlage öffnen" leads back to the template. Each section is always a rendered editor for Markdown (`markdoc-md-editor/markdown-editor`), with no separate view and edit modes: bold, lists and headings show as they render, headings at the size of the section title, and cited statements are underlined; a click on one opens its sources, and while typing ⌘/Ctrl-click does. An empty section shows its template text as placeholder, and the box grows with it. The privacy notice under the Quellen tabs is an amber strip with theme text that reads in light and dark mode. Proposals still show as a word diff with their citations; a citation stays as long as its exact text is in the section. `MarkdownDiffEditor` is reduced to `SuggestionDiff`, the proposal diff.
- Templates mark instructions for the AI with `{% ai %}…{% /ai %}`, inline or around paragraphs. The template page shows them muted and leaves them out of copied text, together with a line that held nothing else. In the editor, typing `((…))` turns the text into an instruction, and the toolbar's magenta "KI" tag button marks a selection. An instruction around several paragraphs stays one block in the editor and is saved as one. The prompts still accept the older `((…))` syntax. While they are tested, `ai` and `section` live in the app (`features/template-tags`), not in markdoc-md: the app passes its extended Markdoc config wherever templates are validated, parsed or rendered, and registers the tags with the template editor through the new `markdoc-md-editor/plugins` registry, which adds a plugin's Markdoc schemas, Tiptap extensions, serializers, checks, toolbar buttons, a footer below the text and help.
- The text input of the case has two fields: the dashed "Hintergrundinformationen" on top, for everything from before the current presentation (previous letters, prior diagnoses, prior findings, long-term medication), and "Notizen" on the current presentation. The previous Diagnoseblock, Anamnese, Befunde and Epikrise fields are removed from AI Scribe, the letter editor and input filling. The model uses the background as a fact source but does not copy it: it reaches the prompt as `<kontext id="background">` after `<notizen id="notes">`, with a rule against copying normal-value lab lists, organisational or private notes, team remarks and discarded suspected diagnoses (on a benchmark of 216 discharge letters this cut copied raw material from 89% to 0–3% of letters). AI Scribe pages in the letter editor show the document on the left and its sources on the right: a "Quellen" box whose icon tabs (Dateien, Audio, Hintergrundinformationen) sit in one row next to its title, on phones above the document. On wider screens the box fills the height of the column, and the Hintergrundinformationen field grows with it. The privacy notice is a red strip under the tabs, where the data is entered, instead of a bar above the document. It shows only Hintergrundinformationen as text, plus Notizen when it already holds text from the case; the document takes the notes. "Generieren" sits in the top bar next to "Text kopieren", and ⌘↵ in the sources also generates. Clicking a cited statement opens the citation dialog with all its sources: the cited passage in the Hintergrundinformationen, or the document page with the cited box. On phones the top bar wraps so the template name stays visible. For a template without sections, "Generieren" sends that text as the draft to rewrite. The MDScribe Agent beside the letter editor is hidden for everyone, the admin included, behind the flag `doctors-note-agent`, and `scribeAgent.chat` refuses requests (FORBIDDEN) while the flag is off. AI Scribe citations point only to evidence: the Hintergrundinformationen and uploaded documents (their OCR text). Notes, the letter's own sections and audio transcripts are not cited, and without any evidence the citation pass does not run. Regenerating one section now sends that section's current text in its slot of `<dokument>` as the draft to rewrite, also from the documentation agent.
- AI-Scribe answers stream immediately and then receive fact-level citations to the evidence inputs, transcripts and OCR text they are based on; clicking an underlined statement opens its sources in the existing citation dialog, including OCR boxes. With a configured decision model, citations come from one decision request instead of a second LLM pass. For each answer item it checks whether all sources together support it (including negation, uncertainty, dates and current versus historical status), scores which source sentences or lines hold its evidence, and keeps attributes such as dose or timing with their fact. Every number or date of a cited fact must appear in its sources. Quotes are literal excerpts, and one statement can cite several sources. In synthetic held-out tests this cost about $0.00024 per answer and 0.2 s instead of about $0.002 and 3.4 s, with no citations on planted unsupported claims. Without a decision model, or if the request fails, the LLM annotation pass is used.
- The Amp feedback widget (open any page with `?amp`) can be enabled by setting `AMP_FEEDBACK_WIDGET_CLIENT_ID`; its script is rendered only for the signed-in admin (`ADMIN_EMAIL`), so other visitors never load it.
- Users can store a personal context (documentation preferences such as abbreviations, style, or a signature line) in a text box under Einstellungen → AI-Scribe. It is sent with every AI-Scribe generation, including custom AI templates and sections the documentation agent regenerates, and with the documentation agent's chat, as `<personal_preferences>` inside the user context. It takes precedence over general style guidance and is never treated as patient data.
- Template pages can be filled directly in the text: info fields are inline inputs with their unit and a compact tooltip showing the full description, switches open an option menu from the whole tag, and calculations open their formula with editable source values. Inline edits go through the same inputs as the side panel, so AI suggestions, provenance and clearing rules stay intact, and copying a selection includes the entered values. Touch devices get 16px inputs to avoid Safari's focus zoom.
- Template pages show no side input column; the bottom toolbar holds the global actions. Every tag occurrence is a Tab stop, and a field reached with Tab selects its content so typing replaces it; choices, calculations and conditions open their details on focus, and tabbing into or clicking a condition focuses its first source field so typing starts right away. Inside a tag's details the arrow keys move between options and fields, while Tab and Shift+Tab always move to the next or previous tag; Esc returns to the tag. Füllen, by button or shortcut, closes the open context panel so the filled text shows. Date fields offer a calendar, conditions list their compared fields and rules, and fields not mentioned in the text are edited inside the tag that uses them. Context, Füllen, "KI-Werte prüfen", "Alle leeren" and Kopieren form a bottom toolbar that always stays one row: on phones its actions are icons, Füllen shows a spinner with a short label while it runs, and undo, redo and "Alle leeren" move into a "⋯" menu; a wide PDF or step bar no longer widens the page. On phones a bar docked at the top edge of the template card names the field and moves to the previous or next tag, so it stays visible above iOS Safari's address bar and keyboard; tablets show the same popovers as desktops. While a field is edited, the surrounding text, including calculations and conditions, keeps its layout and updates when the field is left.
- A click anywhere on an information or calculation tag, including its unit, edits the field. Opening a choice, by Tab or click, focuses its chosen or first option with a visible ring; Space chooses an option and keeps the choice open, Enter chooses it and moves on to the next tag, which may be a field of the chosen text. Details open above a tag when they fit there, so they do not cover the tags that follow.
- Date fields, inline and in the document step bar, take h, g and m for heute, gestern and morgen.
- Füllen shows "Dokumentenerkennung läuft…" while uploaded documents are still being recognized, then the filling progress in the same toast.
- Calculations can be overridden directly in the text, and calculated sources inside another calculation's details can be overridden there; the edit commits when the field is left or on Enter, and clearing it resumes the formula.
- Document pages show one field at a time in a step bar under the PDF instead of the side input column, with previous/next, Enter and Tab to move on. Selecting a date on the PDF focuses its day segment so typing starts right away. Fields follow the PDF's reading order, and checkbox and radio choices are Tab stops too.
- Template and document fields can be undone and redone from the bottom toolbar or with ⌘/Ctrl+Z and ⇧⌘/Ctrl+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Y), also while typing in a field. Consecutive edits of one field, a whole AI fill, and "Alle leeren" are each one step.
- Template and document pages have an "Alle leeren" button in the bottom toolbar that clears every typed value and AI suggestion at once.
- markdoc-md exposes headless tag hooks (`useInfoTag`, `useCalcTag`, `useBranchTag`), typed tag props, and an `onVariableChange` callback on `DynamicMarkdocRenderer`, so applications can supply their own tag components, such as inline inputs, without reimplementing value resolution, rounding, or case selection.
- Feature flags with the Flags SDK, defined in code in `apps/app/flags.ts`. The Template Agent is the first flag and stays admin-only in both the editor and its API.
- Vercel AI Gateway is available as an AI provider, with API-key validation, language-model synchronization, and user-owned keys for admin-exposed connections.
- Cloudflare Workers AI is available as an AI provider. Admins enter their account ID and API token; text generation models are synced from the account catalog and usable in every LLM slot through the OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The account ID is stored as a provider option and the base URL stays a `{accountId}` template. BYOK is unavailable because the connection carries the operator's account ID.
- AI Scribe generates from plain templates: every text template opens at `/aiscribe/<id>`, with one clinical core prompt plus the template's own text, instructions and examples. Templates can be split into `{% section id="…" title="…" %}` blocks, added in the editor with "+ Neuer Abschnitt" below the text, which appends an empty section and puts any text outside the sections into the section before it (on the first click, the whole text becomes the first section) and then focuses the new title; a section is removed again, keeping its text in the section above it (the first one in the section below; a single section becomes plain text), with the ✕ in its header, the "Zusammenführen" handle between two sections (on hover, always on touch screens) or Backspace at its start, each as one undo step offered in a "Rückgängig" toast; a template with two or more sections opens as the letter editor with the documentation agent (formerly Brief-Baukasten), where each section can be edited or regenerated on its own with the other sections as context, and any other template opens the single-text page. Template validation rejects nested sections, duplicate section ids and content outside the sections. Admins choose under Einstellungen → Modelle → AI Scribe which of their collections the AI Scribe overview offers; "Andere Vorlage" leads to the whole library.
- Template tables now use Tiptap's table editor with row/column controls and cell merge/split, saving as built-in Markdoc tables without custom schemas or layout metadata. Cells retain inline formatting and line breaks; merges preserve filled content in one paragraph, and edits that create fully covered rows or unsupported cell blocks are prevented. Rendered templates and rich-text copies include readable table borders and padding.
- OCR previews render Markdown and inert HTML tables with merged cells through one safe renderer and offer image citation-box overlays. Mistral OCR requests HTML tables and integrates separately returned table contents; vision OCR (including OpenRouter Mistral Large 4) preserves model table markup separately from normalized citation geometry.
- Mistral OCR 4 (native document API) and Privatemode DeepSeek OCR 2 (attested, encrypted SDK) can be configured as document models, alongside vision LLMs and self-hosted OCR HTTP services.
- PDF uploads run OCR immediately; image uploads wait for users to manually align and explicitly confirm them in the existing preview before OCR. Progress, errors, retries, extracted text and optional citation-box overlays remain available. Generation reuses the preview's OCR result without uploading the original file again.
- Development seeding makes Mistral OCR and Privatemode OCR selectable as document models when their API keys are available.
- Templates can now include collapsible sections (`{% details %}`) that render like GitHub's collapsed sections: an optional label, `open=true` to expand by default, nesting, and template tags inside. The summary reads like a normal line of text, and a section written directly next to a line (no blank line) continues it without a paragraph gap, so plain lines and expandable lines can alternate. Inserting a section places it directly below the current line and wraps marked content into it; the summary is typed inline on the section's first line, including when an existing section is wrapped, and blank lines next to a section show as empty editor lines. Content in a collapsed section still appears as an input, is included when copying a document, and survives pasting a rendered section back into the editor.
- Registered `@shadcn/lint` with Oxlint and enabled warnings for Tailwind classes that the project cannot generate.
- Templates can compare numbers with `{% condition %}` tags, either for one field (`gt=11`) or for several fields at once (`{% condition ["ivsd", "lvpwd"] %}` with `gt=[11,null]`, where `null` skips a field). All comparisons of a case must match and the first matching case is shown, so separate cases express alternatives. In the template editor, conditions expand inline with a tab per case like switches, while the side panel picks the compared fields and each case's comparisons; the help panel and the Template Agent describe the new tags. Unknown fields a condition compares become number inputs.
- First-run development seeding now configures OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, and Tinfoil providers from available environment API keys, encrypted with the app's auth secret.
- Admins can now create and revise Markdoc templates with an AI agent embedded in the Template Editor sidebar, including audio and file context, while retaining the existing Info view.
- `calc` and numeric `info` tags now support Liquid-style `round` values for configurable decimal places, including `round=false` for unrounded output.
- Calculated template inputs now support nested number, option, and checkbox components, editable overrides, and automatic recalculation.
- Umami now tracks successful signups, logins, AI Scribe generations, template and document creation, and AI-assisted template and document filling.
- AI-filled Markdoc inputs can now carry multiple reusable text or uploaded-file citations, with Cmd/Ctrl-click source previews and explicit mobile source controls. An extensible OCR bounding-box matcher uses the submitted preview result; cited image and PDF pages render locally without another OCR request, with the original file available when a quote cannot be located.
- The file input context now supports photo transfers from an unauthenticated phone via QR code.
- Pasting Markdoc text into the template editor now converts supported input, calc, and switch syntax directly into interactive editor tags.
- Users can now delete their own text templates from the template editor.
- Users can now delete their own PDF document templates from the document editor.
- Switches with the same name form one input offering all of their options. A case's numeric `value` applies to every switch with that name and option, conflicting values are rejected, and each switch still shows only its own text. Checkbox switches count as 1 and 0 in calculations unless a case sets its own value.
- Number info, condition, and calc tags can share one variable. Every named calculation runs even when its section is hidden, and repeated calculations must agree on formula and rounding.
- Admins can select an optional decision model (Entscheidungsmodell) next to the document and audio models. OpenRouter model sync marks Decisions API models (e.g. TypeSafe Jev, Perplexity Decider, Cloudflare Clef), which are selectable only in this slot; development seeding selects Jev 1.14 on a fresh database. With a decision model, text and preprocessed-file autofill (not audio or native-vision files) first asks it once, within 3 seconds, with the same patient context as the standard model and one question per number, switch or boolean field (three for a date: day of the month, month, and year within ±50 years). Number fields choose among every number written in the sources, switches among their options, each with an "other" option; an uncertain date reading is accepted only when that exact date is written in the source. Only answers with at least 0.95 probability and no competing option above 0.05 are accepted, citing the passage that contains the value (among several, the one naming the field). All text fields, uncertain fields and errors go to the standard model, which receives only the remaining fields and is skipped when none remain. The decision model's answers appear in the form immediately while the standard model fills the rest; if that second step fails, the decided values are kept. As soon as autofill finishes, the same model locates all cited passages without OCR boxes in one request per file (about 0.4 s and $0.0004 for 23 quotes instead of one request per click), so opening a citation reuses the prefetched box. Without a decision model, autofill is LLM-only. Fully BYOK requests use it only with the user's own key.
- dots.mocr is available as an alternative self-hosted OCR service on Modal (`services/dotsocr`), returning text and per-block bounding boxes through the same OCR HTTP contract.

### Changed

Refresh the Bun lockfile while keeping incompatible major upgrades (AI SDK 7, TanStack Table 9, Stripe 23, `@types/node` 26, Ultracite 8) deferred.
- Replace the header logo with the lowercase, two-tone mdscribe wordmark and a blue writing cursor, with matching light and dark colors in both the header and its loading state.
- Sections on the template page are cards with a header strip holding the title. The card is drawn with classes only, so copied text still has just the title as a bold line above each part.
- In the tab layout of the input context (letter editor, AI Scribe, Playground), the toolbar with "Brief generieren" sits on top and the open panel below it, as tall as its content instead of at least 68% of the screen.
- "Sammlungen" in the menubar shows only when signed in, like "AI Scribe". In the template editor, visibility is a switch ("Öffentlich" on or off) instead of a select. The template page's text context fields stack at their own height and the panel scrolls, instead of being squeezed until each textarea covered the next label; the unused `stretchFields` option is removed.
- The autofill prompt's context comes from one small composer; the unused selected-template references, prompt-key template fallbacks, FHIR/HL7 source kinds and the date-prefixed context prompt are removed, and the prompt text is unchanged. The phone photo upload sends only its file, and the citation list for editors without inline marks is removed now that letter sections mark citations inline.
- AI-Scribe citations now come from a citation engine (`apps/app/features/citation-engine`) that cites any markdown, or a template with its filled values, against its sources without an LLM: it splits sentences into facts, picks the three closest source passages by trigram (table rows with their header, form labels with their values, long OCR paragraphs as sentences), asks the decision model one yes/no question per passage and whether each fact is written in the sources or is the writer's interpretation, requires every number of a fact in its quote and cites the value after an ungrounded label such as an invented date. Only written facts are marked: conclusions such as "Akutes Delir" from "zunehmende Verwirrtheit" or "Septischer Schock" from "Noradrenalin" stay unmarked (with Jev, a third of a fact's words must also appear in its quote). Each citation carries its evidence with a text range or a box on the OCR page. On 12 AI-Scribe answers it cited 84–87% of written facts, none of 28 interpretations and none of 50 planted errors, in about 0.25 s and $0.0003–0.0006 per answer. Autofill reads its passages from the engine.
- Autofill and the letter editor's sections cite through the same citation engine. Autofill sends filled values as facts, described by their field label, description and unit, instead of running a second LLM pass, and may cite every field of the input context and uploaded files, which are all case context; the LLM pass remains the fallback without a decision model or when it fails. Letter sections mark their cited statements inline (see the letter editor entry). Mammoth-1 (`baseten/`) needs 40% of a fact's words in its quote: on the same 12 answers it cited 212/250 facts, no interpretation and no planted error, in about 0.7 s and $0.0012 per answer.
- Opening a citation scrolls its source to the cited passage. Document pages fill the dialog width so their text stays readable, and the cited box is scrolled into view.
- Remove the experimental PaddleOCR CPU/Modal services, automatic provider seeding, and orb model downloads. Keep generic OCR HTTP support and move the helpers still used by dots.mocr into its own service. Old development snapshots remove the retired provider and its dedicated caches.
- Inline template fields and the document step bar are available to everyone instead of only to the admin; the `inline-inputs` flag and the side input column beside the template and document previews are removed.
- Refresh compatible dependencies, including Next.js 16.4.5, Tiptap 3.33, LiteParse 2.18, AI SDK providers, Better Auth 1.7.18, Postgres.js 4.1, and validation tools. Upgrade Lucide to 1.70, React-PDF to 11 with matching PDF.js 6.3.289, and Hook Form Resolvers to 7. Preserve PDF loading behavior and rotated field highlights with the updated APIs, retain the GitHub brand icon locally, and refresh the Bun lockfile while keeping incompatible major upgrades deferred.
- AI Vorlagen, Brief-Baukästen and the six built-in AI Scribe modes are replaced by templates. `scribeStream` and the agent's `generateSection` take only a `templateId`; the prompt harnesses, their per-mode prompts and the unused `ai-elements` prompt input (with the `nanoid` dependency) are removed, and the scribe-form and workspace routers, their admin and profile editors, and the private-AI-Vorlage entitlement are gone. Each AI Vorlage became a template with the same id and each Brief-Baukasten one sectioned template, by a one-time admin conversion that only copied what the new author could see; the conversion, the one-click creation of the "AI Scribe Standard" collection and the built-in fallback templates were removed after they ran on production. Old URLs (`/aiscribe/er`, `/icu`, `/outpatient`, `/procedures`, `/discharge`, `/diagnoseblock`, `/aiscribe/custom/…`, `/aiscribe/workspace/…`) redirect to the converted or matching standard template, if the visitor can see it; once a converted template is deleted, its old URL leads to `/aiscribe`. The `AiScribeFormConfig` and `AiScribeWorkspace` tables stay until the redirects can go. Migration `0038_ai_scribe_default_collection` adds the admin's default collection to `AiDefaults`.
- The admin Playground works like the app: pick a template, enter text, audio and files in the shared input context, review or edit the prompt `composeTemplatePrompt` builds, and run it against several models at once. A usage event opens with its template, input, model and parameters. `admin.scribe` keeps only `run`, which takes the prompt messages; `compilePrompt`, `prompts` and `transcribeAudio` are gone. The model picker lists only chat models, not OCR or decision models.
- AI-Scribe answers locate their document citations in the background as soon as citations are complete, like Fill Inputs, so opening an image or PDF citation no longer waits for a decision-model request. Both views share one location cache and prefetch in `useCitationPreview`. A decision model that rejects box questions (for example Mammoth-1) shows the cited page without a highlight instead of an error, and is not asked again on every click; rate limits and outages still retry when the citation is opened.
