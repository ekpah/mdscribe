# Changelog

## [Unpublished]

### Added

- AI-Scribe answers stream immediately and then receive fact-level citations to the typed inputs, transcripts and OCR text they are based on; clicking an underlined statement opens its sources in the existing citation dialog, including OCR boxes. With a configured decision model, citations come from one decision request instead of a second LLM pass. For each answer item it checks whether all sources together support it (including negation, uncertainty, dates and current versus historical status), scores which source sentences or lines hold its evidence, and keeps attributes such as dose or timing with their fact. Every number or date of a cited fact must appear in its sources. Quotes are literal excerpts, and one statement can cite several sources. In synthetic held-out tests this cost about $0.00024 per answer and 0.2 s instead of about $0.002 and 3.4 s, with no citations on planted unsupported claims. Without a decision model, or if the request fails, the LLM annotation pass is used.
- The Amp feedback widget (open any page with `?amp`) can be enabled by setting `AMP_FEEDBACK_WIDGET_CLIENT_ID`; its script is rendered only for the signed-in admin (`ADMIN_EMAIL`), so other visitors never load it.
- Users can store a personal context (documentation preferences such as abbreviations, style, or a signature line) in a text box under Einstellungen → AI-Scribe. It is sent with every AI-Scribe generation, including custom AI templates and sections the documentation agent regenerates, and with the documentation agent's chat, as `<personal_preferences>` inside the user context. It takes precedence over general style guidance and is never treated as patient data.
- Template pages can be filled directly in the text: info fields are inline inputs with their unit and a compact tooltip showing the full description, switches open an option menu from the whole tag, and calculations open their formula with editable source values. Inline edits go through the same inputs as the side panel, so AI suggestions, provenance and clearing rules stay intact, and copying a selection includes the entered values. Touch devices get 16px inputs to avoid Safari's focus zoom.
- Template pages show no side input column; the bottom toolbar holds the global actions. Every tag occurrence is a Tab stop, and a field reached with Tab selects its content so typing replaces it; choices, calculations and conditions open their details on focus, and tabbing into or clicking a condition focuses its first source field so typing starts right away. Inside a tag's details the arrow keys move between options and fields, while Tab and Shift+Tab always move to the next or previous tag; Esc returns to the tag. Füllen, by button or shortcut, closes the open context panel so the filled text shows. Date fields offer a calendar, conditions list their compared fields and rules, and fields not mentioned in the text are edited inside the tag that uses them. Context, Füllen, "KI-Werte prüfen", "Alle leeren" and Kopieren form a bottom toolbar; on phones its actions are icons only and wrap onto a second row instead of overlapping, and a wide PDF or step bar no longer widens the page. On phones a bar above the keyboard names the field and moves to the previous or next tag; tablets show the same popovers as desktops. While a field is edited, the surrounding text, including calculations and conditions, keeps its layout and updates when the field is left.
- A click anywhere on an information or calculation tag, including its unit, edits the field. Opening a choice, by Tab or click, focuses its chosen or first option with a visible ring; Space chooses an option and keeps the choice open, Enter chooses it and moves on to the next tag, which may be a field of the chosen text. Details open above a tag when they fit there, so they do not cover the tags that follow.
- Date fields, inline and in the document step bar, take h, g and m for heute, gestern and morgen.
- Füllen shows "Dokumentenerkennung läuft…" while uploaded documents are still being recognized, then the filling progress in the same toast.
- Calculations can be overridden directly in the text, and calculated sources inside another calculation's details can be overridden there; the edit commits when the field is left or on Enter, and clearing it resumes the formula.
- Document pages show one field at a time in a step bar under the PDF instead of the side input column, with previous/next, Enter and Tab to move on. Selecting a date on the PDF focuses its day segment so typing starts right away. Fields follow the PDF's reading order, and checkbox and radio choices are Tab stops too.
- Template and document fields can be undone and redone from the bottom toolbar or with ⌘/Ctrl+Z and ⇧⌘/Ctrl+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Y), also while typing in a field. Consecutive edits of one field, a whole AI fill, and "Alle leeren" are each one step.
- Template and document pages have an "Alle leeren" button in the bottom toolbar that clears every typed value and AI suggestion at once.
- markdoc-md exposes headless tag hooks (`useInfoTag`, `useCalcTag`, `useBranchTag`), typed tag props, and an `onVariableChange` callback on `DynamicMarkdocRenderer`, so applications can supply their own tag components, such as inline inputs, without reimplementing value resolution, rounding, or case selection.
- Feature flags with the Flags SDK, defined in code in `apps/app/flags.ts`. The Template Agent is the first flag and stays admin-only in both the editor and its API.
- Vercel AI Gateway is available as an AI provider, with API-key validation, language-model synchronization, and user-owned keys for admin-exposed connections.
- Cloudflare Workers AI is available as an AI provider. Admins enter their account ID and API token; text generation models are synced from the account catalog and usable in every LLM slot through the OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The account ID is stored as a provider option and the base URL stays a `{accountId}` template. BYOK is unavailable because the connection carries the operator's account ID.
- Template tables now use Tiptap's table editor with row/column controls and cell merge/split, saving as built-in Markdoc tables without custom schemas or layout metadata. Cells retain inline formatting and line breaks; merges preserve filled content in one paragraph, and edits that create fully covered rows or unsupported cell blocks are prevented. Rendered templates and rich-text copies include readable table borders and padding.
- OCR previews render Markdown and inert HTML tables with merged cells through one safe renderer and offer image citation-box overlays. Mistral OCR requests HTML tables and integrates separately returned table contents; vision OCR (including OpenRouter Mistral Large 4) preserves model table markup separately from normalized citation geometry.
- Mistral OCR 4 (native document API) and Privatemode DeepSeek OCR 2 (attested, encrypted SDK) can be configured as document models, alongside vision LLMs and self-hosted OCR HTTP services.
- PDF uploads run OCR immediately; image uploads wait for users to manually align and explicitly confirm them in the existing preview before OCR. Progress, errors, retries, extracted text and optional citation-box overlays remain available. Generation reuses the preview's OCR result without uploading the original file again.
- Development seeding makes Mistral OCR and Privatemode OCR selectable as document models when their API keys are available.
- Templates can now include collapsible sections (`{% details %}`) that render like GitHub's collapsed sections: an optional label, `open=true` to expand by default, nesting, and template tags inside. The summary reads like a normal line of text, and a section written directly next to a line (no blank line) continues it without a paragraph gap, so plain lines and expandable lines can alternate. Inserting a section places it directly below the current line and wraps marked content into it; the summary is typed inline on the section's first line, including when an existing section is wrapped, and blank lines next to a section show as empty editor lines. Content in a collapsed section still appears as an input, is included when copying a document, and survives pasting a rendered section back into the editor.
- Registered `@shadcn/lint` with Oxlint and enabled warnings for Tailwind classes that the project cannot generate.
- Templates can compare numbers with `{% condition %}` tags, either for one field (`gt=11`) or for several fields at once (`{% condition ["ivsd", "lvpwd"] %}` with `gt=[11,null]`, where `null` skips a field). All comparisons of a case must match and the first matching case is shown, so separate cases express alternatives. In the template editor, conditions expand inline with a tab per case like switches, while the side panel picks the compared fields and each case's comparisons; the help panel and the Template Agent describe the new tags. Unknown fields a condition compares become number inputs.
- First-run development seeding now configures OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, and Tinfoil providers from available environment API keys, encrypted with the app's auth secret.
- Admins can now create and revise Markdoc templates with an AI agent embedded in the Template Editor sidebar, including audio and file context, while retaining the existing Info view.
- `calc` and numeric `info` tags now support Liquid-style `round` values for configurable decimal places, including `round=false` for unrounded output.
- Calculated template inputs now support nested number, option, and checkbox components, editable overrides, and automatic recalculation.
- Umami now tracks successful signups, logins, AI Scribe generations, template and document creation, and AI-assisted template and document filling.
- AI-filled Markdoc inputs can now carry multiple reusable text or uploaded-file citations, with Cmd/Ctrl-click source previews and explicit mobile source controls. An extensible OCR bounding-box matcher uses the submitted preview result; cited image and PDF pages render locally without another OCR request, with the original file available when a quote cannot be located.
- The file input context now supports photo transfers from an unauthenticated phone via QR code.
- Pasting Markdoc text into the template editor now converts supported input, calc, and switch syntax directly into interactive editor tags.
- Users can now delete their own text templates from the template editor; dependent AI templates are removed and affected Brief-Baukasten sections revert to the default AI template.
- Users can now delete their own PDF document templates from the document editor.
- Switches with the same name form one input offering all of their options. A case's numeric `value` applies to every switch with that name and option, conflicting values are rejected, and each switch still shows only its own text. Checkbox switches count as 1 and 0 in calculations unless a case sets its own value.
- Number info, condition, and calc tags can share one variable. Every named calculation runs even when its section is hidden, and repeated calculations must agree on formula and rounding.
- Admins can select an optional decision model (Entscheidungsmodell) next to the document and audio models. OpenRouter model sync marks Decisions API models (e.g. TypeSafe Jev, Perplexity Decider, Cloudflare Clef), which are selectable only in this slot; development seeding selects Jev 1.14 on a fresh database. With a decision model, text and preprocessed-file autofill (not audio or native-vision files) first asks it once, within 3 seconds, with the same patient context as the standard model and one question per number, switch or boolean field (three for a date: day of the month, month, and year within ±50 years). Number fields choose among every number written in the sources, switches among their options, each with an "other" option; an uncertain date reading is accepted only when that exact date is written in the source. Only answers with at least 0.95 probability and no competing option above 0.05 are accepted, citing the passage that contains the value (among several, the one naming the field). All text fields, uncertain fields and errors go to the standard model, which receives only the remaining fields and is skipped when none remain. The decision model's answers appear in the form immediately while the standard model fills the rest; if that second step fails, the decided values are kept. As soon as autofill finishes, the same model locates all cited passages without OCR boxes in one request per file (about 0.4 s and $0.0004 for 23 quotes instead of one request per click), so opening a citation reuses the prefetched box. Without a decision model, autofill is LLM-only. Fully BYOK requests use it only with the user's own key.
- dots.mocr is available as an alternative self-hosted OCR service on Modal (`services/dotsocr`), returning text and per-block bounding boxes through the same OCR HTTP contract.

### Changed

- Remove the experimental PaddleOCR CPU/Modal services, automatic provider seeding, and orb model downloads. Keep generic OCR HTTP support and move the helpers still used by dots.mocr into its own service. Old development snapshots remove the retired provider and its dedicated caches.
- Inline template fields and the document step bar are available to everyone instead of only to the admin; the `inline-inputs` flag and the side input column beside the template and document previews are removed.
- Refresh compatible dependencies, including Next.js 16.4.5, Tiptap 3.33, LiteParse 2.18, AI SDK providers, Better Auth 1.7.18, Postgres.js 4.1, and validation tools. Upgrade Lucide to 1.70, React-PDF to 11 with matching PDF.js 6.3.289, and Hook Form Resolvers to 7. Preserve PDF loading behavior and rotated field highlights with the updated APIs, retain the GitHub brand icon locally, and refresh the Bun lockfile while keeping incompatible major upgrades deferred.
- AI-Scribe answers locate their document citations in the background as soon as citations are complete, like Fill Inputs, so opening an image or PDF citation no longer waits for a decision-model request. Both views share one location cache and prefetch in `useCitationPreview`. A decision model that rejects box questions (for example Mammoth-1) shows the cited page without a highlight instead of an error, and is not asked again on every click; rate limits and outages still retry when the citation is opened.
- Tab skips a condition when each field it compares is already a field in the text, as in a row of readings that conditions grade; Tab then moves straight from field to field. A click still opens the condition.
- Fill Inputs allows its citation pass 20 s instead of 8 s within the existing 30 s evidence budget. Filled values still appear before their sources.
- Agents now rely on CI's production Docker build job instead of running the same build in their orb. CI no longer posts a PR summary comment, which only repeated GitHub's check results, and checks out only the latest commit. Pushes to `main` now deploy to Coolify only after every CI check passes, through Coolify's deploy webhook.
- The app uses Inter, self-hosted through `next/font` (no requests to Google), instead of each device's system font, so text and layout render the same on every operating system and in automated screenshots.
- Refresh compatible workspace dependencies, including AI SDK 6 providers, Better Auth 1.7.14, Tiptap 3.32.2, React Query 5.105, React Hook Form 7.91, Markdoc 0.5.11, FHIRPath 5.2.3, Nodemailer 10.3.1, and OXC tooling. Upgrade Postgres.js from 3 to 4 while preserving JavaScript parameter inference, and refresh esbuild/ProseMirror overrides and the Bun lockfile. Update OCR multipart parsing to 0.0.33 and align Modal's HTTP/image dependencies with the CPU service, retaining compatible inference-framework pins.
- Update compatible dependencies to Next.js 16.4.3, AI SDK 6.0.315, Better Auth 1.7.12, oRPC 1.16.1, LiteParse 2.17, Privatemode 1.60, Tinfoil 1.3, Nodemailer 10.2.1, and current supporting tools. Upgrade Hook Form Resolvers to 6, Nano ID to 6, FastAPI to 0.162, and Pillow to 13 while retaining incompatible major lines and PaddleOCR's NumPy constraint. Declare Tinfoil's patched Node.js requirements, remove redundant SMTP types, and adapt fixtures and unused catch parameters to the updated linter.
- Mistral OCR now uses `mistral-ocr-4-1`, because Mistral retired OCR 4.0. Existing connections switch over by refreshing the provider's models in the admin settings and reselecting the default model.
- Field values, AI suggestions and their provenance live in a `useTemplateFields` hook shared by the side form, inline tags and document step bar.
- Removed the favourites feature in favour of collections. A migration moves each user's favourites into a new "Favoriten" collection and drops the `_favourites` table. The template sidebar opens the first collection by default, the dashboard lists collections instead of favourites, editor category suggestions draw on collection templates, and favourite counts are gone from the sidebar, dashboard, and admin pages.
- AI models now have a `kind` (`chat`, `decision`, `extraction`, `ocr`) instead of the `supportsDecisions` flag, and each default slot only accepts its kinds. Models also store input modalities, context window, max output tokens, and a pi-ai-compatible `cost` (token rates per million, request-wide tiers, plus optional per-request, per-image, per-audio-minute and per-page prices); OpenRouter sync fills them from its catalog. Provider and model settings live in `options` JSON (the OpenRouter routing mode moved there), and `{key}` placeholders in a provider's base URL are filled from its options. Migration `0035_model_config` backfills existing rows. Model rows are unique per provider, model ID and kind, so one model can be both a chat and a decision model.
- The decision model (Entscheidungsmodell) can now also be Cloudflare Clef or Clef Flash via Workers AI, or OpenAI's Decisions API (`gpt-6-luna`, synced as a separate decision entry). Clef requests are split into batches of 64 questions, and their cost is computed from the model's input price. Locating citations on document images still requires an OpenRouter decision model, because only its Decisions API answers box questions.
- In the template editor, marked text inserted as a Switch or Condition now becomes the content of the first case instead of the tag's name, keeping formatting and embedded tags; Info and Calc still use it as the name.
- Update compatible dependencies to Next.js 16.4.2, React 19.3.1, Tiptap 3.32, AI SDK 6.0.309, Better Auth 1.7.10, Nodemailer 10.1.2, FastAPI 0.143.3, and current supporting libraries and tools. Preserve the three-day npm release-age policy, shared catalogs, hoisted lockfile, existing runtime/peer-compatible major versions, and PaddleX's NumPy constraint. Bound local SMTP test-server shutdown for the updated email dependencies.
- Fill Inputs now shows decision-model values first, generates remaining values with a short, inference-friendly prompt without requiring quotes, and adds source-passage citations afterward without rewriting values. Citation failures retain the filled form and show a warning; native-vision files are transcribed only in the later evidence phase when no OCR text exists.
- Fill Inputs now defers final model usage logging through Next.js `after()`, matching decision-model logging, so logging failures do not prevent completed field suggestions from reaching the client.
- Images can start OCR directly from their file row, which shows a spinner while OCR runs, a check when it is complete, and the red retry arrow after an error. Opening an image still lets users rotate it before starting OCR.
- Refresh workspace and PaddleOCR dependencies, including Next.js 16.4, React 19.3, Tiptap 3.31, Nodemailer 10, Marked 18, and Diff 9. Keep AI SDK 6, TanStack Table 8, React-PDF 10, Stripe 22, Nano ID 5, and Lucide 0.x for existing API, peer, runtime, and icon compatibility. Align Node types with Node 24 and deduplicate Ajv and ProseMirror through overrides.
- Refresh transitive dependencies while explicitly preserving Bun's hoisted workspace resolution. Deduplicate React for Mint's CLI, remove the incompatible blanket js-yaml 4 override, and add nullable Stripe subscription lifecycle and schedule columns required by Better Auth, with an additive migration and existing null-to-undefined normalization.
- Vision LLM OCR now returns text only through compact adapter-private output keys; `OcrResult` geometry is optional. Cited passages without OCR boxes are located by the configured decision model in one batched request after autofill, or when a source is opened before that finishes; uncertain matches show the original page without a highlight. Table grids, empty transcription cells and selection labels remain explicit.
- Orb review portals now build with Bun/Turbopack and serve production snapshots with Bun instead of running a persistent dev server. Bun is pinned to 1.3.14 in orb setup, package-manager metadata, and CI to match the Docker runtime; Webpack dev mode remains available on demand for agent iteration.
- Stripe subscriptions are now optional. Instances without a Stripe secret key run on the free usage limit and no longer show pricing, upgrade, or billing controls.
- Switches are now selections or checkboxes only: number switches and switches over several fields are rejected in favor of `condition`, and every calc needs a name. Template saves validate the complete template.
- Calculations show a result only once all of their values are filled in. Until then, the calc, any Info showing its value, and its input field show „…“ instead of 0 or NaN; calculations that depend on it wait as well, and conditions on it use their default case. Unchecked checkboxes count as filled.
- Manual input now takes precedence over AI suggestions, which take precedence over calculations. Each successful autofill replaces all earlier AI suggestions while keeping manual values, and resetting a calculated field shows its current AI suggestion or calculation again.
- Template tags now use compact, subtly tinted chips with text-style deletion and isolated selection; Info and Calc chips show units when `renderUnit` is enabled. Their inspectors show shared attributes first, including calc descriptions. Switches expand directly within the document with option tabs and a collapse button, preserving surrounding text and sharing one toolbar at the top; tag settings stay on the right. Chip and expanded-switch colors are defined centrally. A calc input that is itself calculated elsewhere mirrors that calculation's editable value, including its override state, and links to it with “berechnet ↗”; dependent calculations resolve in dependency order.
- The Template Agent now reads and independently edits content, examples, and information, preserving untouched sections and line breaks and explaining reuse of named calculations. Clicking a template tag opens the Info sidebar, including already-selected tags, without switching views during ordinary edits.
- A selected document model now enables OCR and takes precedence over native vision in generation, autofill, and agents. OCR submissions contain only `kind`, `name`, an optional stable citation `id`, and a structurally validated `OcrResult` with text and optional bounding boxes, without original bytes, MIME type, or file size. Usage summaries measure the submitted OCR JSON rather than the original file. OCR result signing, expiry, and model binding are removed: submission reuses the preview result as user-provided context without another OCR request. Without a document model, OCR controls and requests are absent and native vision receives the original file.
- OCR preprocessing keeps line-level page bounding boxes from engines that provide them in the returned OCR result; Scribe's prompt receives only the indexed document text with its source reference. Uploaded files can be opened with the shared app-level PDF preview and toggled to their OCR text after preprocessing.

- Moved app-specific development seeding and API-key encryption into the app, while keeping schema setup and migrations in the database package.
- Scribe document OCR now runs through a single `extractOcrDocument` entry point with one adapter per engine (vision LLM, OCR HTTP, Mistral, Privatemode). Automatic orientation detection/correction is removed: manual image alignment before OCR is an explicit product requirement, with any boxes in the supplied page frame. Page bounding boxes are logged in per-file OCR usage events (except under ZDR). The unused admin `ocrToMarkdown` endpoint and the document-model "direct/multimodal" mode were removed.
- Reworked the markdoc-md README into a progressive medical-template tutorial, with a separate packaged language and integration reference.
- The dashboard usage card now identifies active BYOK connections while continuing to show the MDScribe quota consumed by requests using other models.
- Renamed the Markdoc `score` tag to `calc`, while retaining `score` as a backward-compatible alias across parsing, rendering, the editor, and documentation.

- Split the generic design primitives, Markdoc TipTap editor, and app-owned input renderer into explicit package boundaries.
- Values manually entered exactly as suggested by AI now retain a green bot indicator; diverging edits remain reversible to the AI suggestion.
- Aligned the landing-page feature previews with their vertical scroll interaction, including right-side pagination, consistent clinical titles, and matching inline vital-sign examples.
- Reworked the landing page copy to address doctors instead of developers: replaced technical terms like "Markdown" and "Markdoc" with "Textbausteine" throughout hero, feature sections, demo labels, and metadata.
